<span style="font-size:0.9em;">
I am a PostDoc at King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST) in [Stochastic Processes and Applied Statistics Research Group](https://stochproc.kaust.edu.sa/). </span> 

<span style="font-size:0.9em;">
My research lies at the intersection of computational probability, mathematical statistics, and numerical analysis. Specifically, I study and develop rigorous and computationally efficient methods for stochastic differential equations (SDEs), stochastic partial differential equations (SPDEs), and latent variable models. A central question in my work is how to extract statistical information from these models while controlling the errors and computational costs introduced by their approximation. These difficulties arise from intractable likelihoods, time and space discretization, and the need to approximate distributions. To address them, I combine tools from stochastic analysis, functional analysis, and Monte Carlo methods. My research connects the mathematical analysis of stochastic models with the construction and implementation of algorithms for inference, sampling, and uncertainty quantification.
